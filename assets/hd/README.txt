HD images for Stunt Car Racer (cockpit, engine, flames, wheels, crane...) by Popov2026, distributed
with this project. They replace the original cockpit sprites in HD mode (see native/README_HD.txt).
Copied into the hd folder of the Windows release and loaded by the browser version.
foule.wav: royalty-free crowd sound, looped by the grandstands instead of the synthesized crowd.
